What is Wordora?

Wordora is a free daily word-guessing game inspired by classic word puzzles. Each day, a new 5-letter mystery word is revealed. You get six chances to guess it. After each guess, the tiles change colour to guide you closer to the answer.

Green means the letter is correct and in the right position. Yellow means the letter is in the word but in the wrong place. Grey means the letter isn't in the word at all.

Tips & Strategies

Start with a word that covers common vowels and consonants — words like CRANE, STARE, or AUDIO are great openers. Pay attention to yellow tiles: the letter is in the word, just not where you put it. Use your remaining attempts to narrow down vowel positions before committing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the puzzle change?

A new Wordora puzzle is available every day at midnight UTC. The same puzzle is shared with all players worldwide on the same calendar day.

Can I play previous puzzles?

Currently, Wordora offers one puzzle per day. Previous puzzles are not available to replay, which keeps the community sharing the same experience each day.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — your current game progress and statistics are saved in your browser. If you refresh the page, your guesses will be restored. Progress is stored locally and is not synced between devices.

What counts as a valid guess?

Your guess must be an existing 5-letter English word from our approved word list. Proper nouns, abbreviations, and random letter strings are not accepted.

How does the streak work?

Your streak increments each consecutive day you solve the puzzle. Missing a day resets your current streak to zero, but your maximum streak is preserved.

Is Wordora free?

Wordora is completely free to play. No account, download, or payment required.
